A line with an area fill draws as ECharts does. It stays a line, with its stroke and symbols drawn over the fill; before, it was an opaque polygon with no line. The fill's opacity, colour and origin (`auto` closes to zero, or the nearer edge; `start`, `end` or a value) are configurable, so a range through zero closes to the zero line instead of the floor.
