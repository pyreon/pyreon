Every published package declares the supported Node.js floor: `"engines": { "node": ">=22" }`. 73 packages declared no `engines`, 13 said `>= 22` and the seven `@pyreon/compiler-*` binaries still said `>=18`. Node.js 22 is the floor of the support matrix (Node.js 22+, Bun 1.3+, browsers at Baseline 2024, iOS 17+, Android minSdk 26+), now stated on the stability page and in SECURITY.md; the new `check-support-matrix` gate keeps every package, the native platform floors and both documents in agreement, so a new package cannot ship without the field.
