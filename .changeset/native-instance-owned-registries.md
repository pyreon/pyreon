Plugin registries are instance-owned and reach the parser and emitters. `createCompiler` builds one `CompilerRegistries` (`services`, derived tables, `elements`) per instance from its built-in, discovered and explicit plugins, exposed as `compiler.registries`; the parser and both emitters read it through a scoped slot that `transform` installs for the duration of parse + passes + emit and restores in `finally` (re-entrant, exception-safe). `CompilerPlugin` gains an additive `elements` field (API version stays 1): a plugin's element lowerings and `services` are now lowered end to end on both targets, a hook is claimed when imported from `@pyreon/*` or a plugin's `modules`, and a `(module, tag)` pair or hook claimed by two owners is a load-time error naming both. The built-in services, `@pyreon/elements` and `@pyreon/coolgrid` lowerings are `builtIn` plugins registered through the same path (replaceable by name); the process-global `registerElementLowering`, `SERVICE_BY_HOOK` and `NATIVE_LOWERED_HOOKS` are gone (`nativeLoweredHooks()`, `createElementRegistry`, `parsePyreon(..., { registries })` replace them). Emitted Swift/Kotlin for the built-ins is byte-identical.
