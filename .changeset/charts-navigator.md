`<PlotChart navigator>` — the slider dataZoom: a strip under the plot shows the first series over ALL rows with the zoom window as a band; drag the band to move the window, drag a handle to resize it (window math shared with the wheel/pan zoom, minimum span enforced). Works with or without the inside `dataZoom`; the strip rect is exposed as `data-pyreon-nav`.
