Option line and scatter labels sit where ECharts puts them: a line's above its symbol, a scatter point's inside it, both against the symbol's box and with the same automatic colours as bar labels. A line with `showSymbol: false` shows no labels, as in ECharts. Scatter points default to ECharts' `symbolSize` of 10 (they were drawn at 6).
