Fifteen more service hooks move onto the data-driven `services.ts` descriptors: `useClipboard`, `useBluetooth`, `useWakeLock`, `useDeviceInfo`, `useSafeArea`, `useScreenOrientation`, `useDeviceMotion`, `useSpeech`, `useAudioRecorder`, `useOnline`, `useAppState`, `useCrashReporter`, `useGeolocation`, `usePush` and `usePayments`. Their satellite behaviour (accessor reads that become properties, a bare-call read, Compose `.value` members, Swift start/stop lifecycle, destructure aliasing, optional-field typing) is now a small data vocabulary on the descriptor instead of per-hook module-level name sets, recognizer branches, `DeclIR` variants and emit branches. The compiled Swift/Kotlin for every source in the golden corpus is byte-identical, including the synthesized `__Obj0_<hash>` struct names.

Observable changes: the exported `DeclIR` type loses the kinds `network-status`, `app-state`, `crash-reporter`, `geolocation`, `push`, `payments`, `clipboard`, `bluetooth`, `wake-lock`, `device-info`, `safe-area`, `screen-orientation`, `device-motion`, `speech` and `audio-recorder` (use `{ kind: 'service', hook }`). The per-binding name sets are now per component rather than file-scoped, so a binding name in one component can no longer rewrite a same-named value in another. A Compose `MutableState` member (`bt.devices`, `m.active`, `net.isOnline()`) now reads `.value` in both its member and zero-argument call forms; previously the hardware services' member form and the connectivity services' call form emitted the bare state object. `useBluetooth`/`useWakeLock`/`useDeviceInfo`/`useSafeArea`/`useScreenOrientation`/`useDeviceMotion`/`useSpeech`/`useAudioRecorder`/`useCamera` now also warn when declared inside a `<For>`/`<Show>` render callback, like every other service hook.
