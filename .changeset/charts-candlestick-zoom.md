`<CandlestickChart>` gains a `zoom` prop (`CandlestickZoom`, every field optional). The `slider` draws ECharts' strip under the chart; drag the band or a handle to move the window. `inside` zooms on the wheel and pans on a drag inside the plot. `window` sets the opening window, and `lock`, `minSpan` and `maxSpan` hold. A click still reports the GLOBAL candle index.
